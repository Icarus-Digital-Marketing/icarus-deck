Pillar04

Lifecycle and Email Retention Add-on

The Retention OS. A seven-layer cross-brand operating system that turns commerce data into lifecycle flows, segments, copy, design, deployment and self-improving optimisation across email, SMS, WhatsApp and on-site chat. Built and proven on VERO; designed to multiply to any brand without code changes.

Open
The seven layers
  1. L0Brand and data intake. Site, PDPs, FAQs, reviews, science pages, founder voice, brand rules. Commerce events from Medusa or your store: orders, customers, subscriptions, refunds. Support data: tickets, chat logs, objection mining.
  2. L1Intelligence and customer state. A ten-state customer state machine (Anonymous, Lead, Quiz-complete, Protocol-considered, Carted, First-buyer, Active, At-risk, Lapsed, Suppressed). Four overlay flags (subscriber, advocate, vip, won-back). Eight segmentation dimensions (goal, protocol affinity, value tier, purchase format, replenishment window, engagement, acquisition source, channel consent).
  3. L2Strategy engine. Lifecycle blueprint. Continuity and advocacy logic (replenishment, subscription, UGC, referrals). Channel and fatigue policy with frequency caps, priority, mutual exclusion. ESP-agnostic. Brand-aware. Machine-readable JSON per flow.
  4. L3Two content engines. Copy engine (email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat scripts) bound by a runtime-generated compliance gate. Design engine — Claude Code scene-stack pipeline assembling premium scenes into React Email modules with ESP-safe HTML output. Banned terms parse out of the brand spec at run time, never hardcoded.
  5. L4Multi-channel deployers. Email via Brevo, Klaviyo, or Omnisend. SMS via Brevo SMS or Twilio. WhatsApp via BSP. On-site chat via your widget. Same strategy plus content, different rails.
  6. L5Execution and event capture. Live performance data: opens, clicks, revenue per message, repeat rate, reviews, subscription state, support themes. Every event writes back into one Supabase metrics store. One source of truth for the optimiser.
  7. L6Self-improving optimisation loop. Three sub-loops: performance analyser (surfaces weak nodes, dropoffs, fatigue), rules updater (adjusts segments, transitions, channel mix, frequency), content updater (rotates copy tests, refreshes proof from live support and review data).
Twelve lifecycle flows
  • Welcome (pre-quiz). Email capture, no quiz. 4 emails / 10 days. Goal: quiz completion.
  • Welcome (post-quiz). Quiz complete, no purchase. 5 emails / 14 days. Goal: first order.
  • Abandoned protocol. Result viewed, no PDP add. 3 emails / 72 hr.
  • Abandoned cart. Cart, no checkout. 3 emails / 48 hr.
  • Post-purchase onboarding. 6 emails / 30 days. Adherence plus subscribe.
  • Replenishment. Day 25 of 30-day supply. Reorder or subscription upgrade.
  • Subscriber VIP. Monthly. Retention plus cross-protocol.
  • Win-back. Lapsed 60 days. 3 emails / 14 days.
  • Sunset. No engagement 120 days. 2 emails. Suppression.
  • Advocacy and review request. Day 21 post-purchase. UGC plus reviews.
  • Field Notes broadcast. Weekly. Authority and retention.
  • Protocol drop broadcast. Per launch. Sales spike.
