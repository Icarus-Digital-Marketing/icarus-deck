Pillar01

SEO architecture

An agentic SEO architecture that ships category-leading content nightly and refires it on a freshness gate. Run by a 13-stage publish-and-audit loop that closes the gap most agencies do not even map.

Open
The publish-and-audit loop
  1. 1Keyword auto-promote. A nightly agent refills the ideation queue against your category before the writer touches it. Without this, the pipeline starves in roughly eight days.
  2. 2Multi-model research. SERP intelligence, competitor mapping, citation harvesting, query intent classification.
  3. 3Voice-locked draft. Generated against your brand-MD: tone rules, compliance constraints, banned phrases, product naming. Off-brand output cannot pass the gate.
  4. 4Fact-check gate. Every statistical claim is checked against the cited source. Broken citations and unverified claims surface before publish.
  5. 5SEO checklist scan. Title length, meta description, header structure, internal-link plan, schema markup, image alt-text.
  6. 6Schema validation. JSON-LD validated live against your published page.
  7. 7Internal-link injection. Surgical bidirectional links across the cluster, no duplicate anchors, no orphan pages.
  8. 8Indexing push. Google Indexing API plus IndexNow to Bing and Yandex on publish.
  9. 9Content quality score. Zero to one hundred across content depth, on-page SEO, E-E-A-T, technical, and AI-citation readiness.
  10. 10Refresh runner. Weekly. Consumes a stale-content plan, refires under-performers, anti-thrash gate at thirty days, post-refire verification catches no-op writes.
  11. 11Monthly Site Health audit. Up to five hundred pages crawled, delegated across fifteen SEO specialist agents, prioritised action queue out the other side.
  12. 12Monthly Article Performance audit. Every article scored, orphans flagged, cannibalisation detected, stale content categorised for the refresh queue.
  13. 13Indexation watchdog. Daily URL inspection. Drift alerts when an article slips out of the index.
What you see
  • A new article live on your site, indexed by Google and Bing, internally linked into the rest of the cluster.
  • A monthly Site Health Score with a prioritised action queue.
  • A monthly Article Performance audit covering every existing page.
  • Weekly cadence report: articles published, refresh count, average quality score.
  • Drift alerts when an article scores below threshold, when a citation breaks, or when schema fails.
Outcome
A category-authority site that compounds its own traffic, with zero copy you would not sign your name to.
First article live by week three. Full audit cadence live by week four.
Pillar02

Email and retention OS

Retention is a state machine, not a campaign. A seven-layer operating system that turns commerce data into lifecycle flows, segments, copy, design, deployment and continuous optimisation across email, SMS, WhatsApp and on-site chat.

Open
The seven layers
  1. L0Brand and data intake. Site, PDPs, FAQs, reviews, commerce events, support tickets, returns data. The signal pool the rest of the OS reads from.
  2. L1Customer intelligence. A ten-state customer state machine with four overlay flags. Eight segmentation dimensions covering goal, protocol affinity, value tier, purchase format, replenishment window, engagement, acquisition source, channel consent.
  3. L2Strategy engine. Lifecycle blueprint (welcome, abandonment, post-purchase, replenishment, win-back, sunset), continuity and advocacy logic, channel and fatigue policy. ESP-agnostic.
  4. L3Content engines. Copy and design split into two engines, both bound by a runtime-generated compliance gate. Banned terms parse out of your brand spec at run time, never hardcoded.
  5. L4ESP deployers. Brevo, Klaviyo, Omnisend rails, plus SMS, WhatsApp and chat. Same strategy, same content, different rails.
  6. L5Event capture. Webhook events flow into a metrics store. One source of truth feeds the optimiser.
  7. L6Self-improving loop. Performance analyser surfaces weak nodes, drop-offs, fatigue. A rules updater adjusts segments, transitions, channel mix and frequency. A content updater rotates copy tests and refreshes proof from live support and review data.
Human review, four checkpoints only
  • Claims risk.
  • Pricing strategy.
  • Final brand sign-off.
  • Edge-case customer logic.
What you see
  • A live lifecycle programme covering twelve flows on average: welcome, abandonment, post-purchase, replenishment, subscriber VIP, win-back, sunset, advocacy, broadcast.
  • Per-flow performance tied to revenue, not vanity metrics.
  • A quarterly retention health review: subscription rate, replenishment compliance, win-back recovery rate, advocacy yield.
  • Approval surfaces for the four human gates only.
Outcome
Lifecycle revenue you can chart against the OS, not against your operators' time. Lift measured in sixty days. Multiplies cleanly to subsequent brands inside a fortnight once proved on the first.
First branded flow live in three to four weeks. Twelve-flow coverage by week six to eight.

Web design and landing pages

An agentic web-build pipeline that turns your brand pack into conversion-engineered landing and sales pages on a locked design system, with motion graphics generated alongside the page rather than as a separate workflow. Ships on Next.js to Vercel under your domain.

Open
The build protocol
Locked design-system contract

Palette, typography, spacing, layout primitives, voice rules. Every agent reads it before emitting code. Off-brand output cannot get through the gate.

Composed-skill architecture

Design taste, frontend architecture, brand guidelines and HyperFrames motion graphics compose into a single page-build skill. Operators invoke one skill; the composition fires automatically.

Two-turn protocol

Turn one emits a greyscale wireframe locking section anatomy. Turn two applies the brand styling layer. A human approval gate sits between them. Drift cannot compound silently.

ConversionWise anatomy

Twenty-three sections in ATIDFA order embedded as a corpus the agent consults section by section. Identity gates enforce logo-only nav, one CTA copy at every position, customer-priority section, transformation framed as outcome not deliverable, three-card bundle layout.

Five-stage post-generation gate

Audit (structural), critique (anti-slop), quieter (decoration strip), normalize (token conformance), polish (small-detail shipping pass). Every section passes all five before the operator sees it.

HyperFrames motion

Hero loops, animated proof blocks and ad-creative variants generated in the same pipeline, conforming to your design tokens.

What you see
  • Production-ready pages on your own domain.
  • A design-system document you keep. Future iterations conform to it.
  • A page-level performance view in the Icarus Dashboard once tracking is live.
Outcome
A page architecture you can grow into, not a one-shot landing page. Subsequent pages on the same brand ship in half the time once the system is locked.

Blueprint-first protocol

Anti-drift contract

Blueprint changes go through a changelog. Reality cannot diverge from the documented system. Operators inherit the same map across sessions and across handovers.

Composed-skill model

Skills inherit from each other and override settings explicitly. Operators invoke one skill; the composition fires automatically. No fragile chains, no silent overrides.

Five-stage gate sequence

Vector-indexed brain

The agency's accumulated wiki, knowledge base, memory and project archives, indexed and retrievable on demand. The librarian retrieves. The agent reasons.

Cost-quality cascade

Heavy reasoning runs on frontier models. Routine work cascades to cheaper, faster models. The right tool for the right job at every step of every pillar.

Two-model code review

Codex review handoff for any meaningful code change. Two-model agreement before ship. Bugs the first model misses, the second one catches.

Per-pillar memory

Context carries cleanly across sessions and operators. The system remembers your brand even when the operator changes seats.

Each pillar has its own feedback loop. SEO has the monthly audit and the refresh runner. Email has L6 optimisation. Paid Media has its cadence reviews. The system gets better while it runs.
