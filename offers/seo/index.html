SEO pays off over 6 to 12 months. Early signs you will see: pages found by Google, and first search appearances.

Do the articles publish themselves?

No. Drafts are written and checked automatically, then held as hidden drafts until a person approves them. That is on purpose.

Can you tell me if ChatGPT recommends us?

Not reliably yet, and we will not pretend. We check Google's AI answers weekly from two sources and Perplexity from one. ChatGPT does not answer automated checks dependably, and our screen says so instead of guessing.

Is this just AI writing?

AI drafts, and it is the smallest part. The value is in what surrounds it: real search demand in, sixteen checks, a person's approval, and a daily and weekly watch after it ships.

How fast will we rank?

SEO is a 6 to 12 month game, and anyone promising page one in a month is selling something else. Your first article goes live in week 2, and you see Google pick pages up long before they rank.
