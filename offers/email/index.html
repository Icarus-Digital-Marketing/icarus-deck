Does it rewrite emails on its own?

No. We pull results every week, and people decide what to change. We chose that on purpose. An email that rewrites itself can also break itself.

Which email platforms do you work with?

Klaviyo and Omnisend. We connect to your account directly, so your lists and history stay where they are.

Our category gets flagged. Can you help us reach the inbox?

Often, yes. For brands that need it, we can send from your own setup. It warms up step by step, from 50 emails a day to 1,000, with an inbox test every week. We price it on the call, because it depends on your volume.

Will you make up offers to push sales?

Never. The writer only uses offers you have confirmed, and the reader test stops any email that promises something your store does not have.

Is this written by AI?

AI drafts. The brief, the separate review, the reader test, the checks and the send button are what make it safe to put in front of your customers.
