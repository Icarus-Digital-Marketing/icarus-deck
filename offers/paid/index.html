The rules that judge your ads, in plain English.

No black box. These are the actual rules our dashboard applies to every ad, measured against your own target cost per sale.

Kill: not connectingUnder 1% of viewers click after 500+ views.
Kill: no interest4+ days, two target costs spent, not one cart.
Wait: too earlyUnder 3 sales and under 200 visits. Numbers that small are noise.
Scale: all three signalsCheap carts, 3+ sales, cost per sale at or under target.
Hold: quiet helperDear per sale, but cheap carts. It feeds the ads that close.
Kill: funnel stuckSales coming in, but each costs over twice the target and carts are dear.
Winner: earned30+ days of spend, or a confirmed scale.

Are your ad accounts pre-approved for my category?

No one can honestly promise that. The platforms review every ad on its own, whoever runs it. What we bring is years of knowing where the lines are in strict categories, writing inside them from the first draft, and planning for reviews instead of being surprised by them.

Do you use AI to make the ads?

Yes, as a tool in our team's hands. It studies proven ads and your product, then makes drafts. People choose, fix and approve. Nothing goes out that a person has not seen.

Why not just trust the numbers in the ad platform?

Because the platform counts its own wins. We match each ad to real visits, carts and orders in your store. Sometimes an ad that looks weak in the platform is quietly filling carts for the others. We keep those.

How fast will I see results?

Your first new ads go live in 3 to 4 weeks. We do not promise a return figure up front, because anyone who does is guessing. We promise you will know which ads are working, and why.

Who do I talk to day to day?

Your account team. They run onboarding, reporting and your monthly strategy review.
